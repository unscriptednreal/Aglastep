Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are the skills labs free?

The practice labs on this website are free and require no login. External course providers may set their own terms.

Does AglaStep give an IQ score or job certificate?

No. These are educational practice questions, not validated intelligence testing, hiring decisions, or accredited certifications.

Does the site save my answers?

Assessments run in the browser. Your answers are not sent to an AglaStep server, and progress does not carry between devices.

Is the Android app available?

The Android application remains in development; these browser tools preview the learning experience.
