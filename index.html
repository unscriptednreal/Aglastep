Good to know

Questions before your next step?

Is the AglaStep app available now?

The Android application is in development. This website previews the proposed experience; a public app launch date hasn't been announced.

Who is AglaStep for?

BPO freshers, entry-level customer-service agents, and mid-level professionals preparing for their next role.

Will AglaStep help me get a job?

Our aim is to help you practise and improve. AglaStep is not a recruitment agency and cannot guarantee interviews, jobs, promotions or certifications.

Will there be free lessons?

We plan to offer introductory learning free of charge. Any paid features would be clearly priced before purchase.

Can I use it on my phone?

The website works on mobile browsers. An Android app is planned, but not yet published.
