What is AglaStep?

AglaStep is an Android career-learning app in development for BPO freshers and entry- to mid-level customer-service professionals. It aims to help with interview preparation, call-handling practice and career development.

Is AglaStep a job placement agency?

No. AglaStep focuses on learning and career preparation. We do not promise jobs, interviews or promotions.

When will the app launch?

We're building and testing the first version. We haven't announced a confirmed release date yet.

Will it be free?

We plan to offer free introductory learning experiences. Any future paid features will be clearly priced before purchase.

Will course recommendations be independent?

Our goal is to recommend learning resources according to career goals and skill needs. Any future paid promotions or affiliate relationships will be disclosed.
